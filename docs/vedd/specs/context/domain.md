# Campaign Map Domain Context

## Campaign structure

The Path of Exile 2 campaign is composed of Acts.

Each Act contains areas.

Areas can connect to other areas.

Connections shown in the companion represent
direct in-game area transitions.

They do NOT represent:
- recommended routes
- quest dependency
- chronological order

unless explicitly encoded separately.

## Areas

An area may contain:

- waypoint
- one or more bosses
- optional encounters
- quests
- permanent rewards
- notable loot/rewards
- points of interest

Not every field is available for every area.

## Boss knowledge

Boss information may include:

- damage types
- resistances
- weaknesses
- attacks/mechanics
- relevant rewards
- Hardcore warnings
